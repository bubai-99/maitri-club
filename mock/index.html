              Privacy & terms

              Last updated 13 November 2026

              What we keep

              The head of family’s name, mobile number and address; family members’ names and relation; card photos and blood group (optional); and your subscription and receipt history.

              Why

              To run membership, receipts, event passes and the AGM accounts. Blood group is only looked at when a blood donation camp needs it.

              Who can see it

              Only committee members who need it for their job: the treasurer sees accounts, volunteers see only gate passes. Nothing is sold or shared outside the club.

              Your choices

              See and change your details in the app’s Settings. If you leave the club, ask and we delete your details, keeping only the accounts the law requires.

              Terms

              Member cards are not transferable. If one is lost, tell the club; the old QR is cancelled and a new card is issued.

              Questions? Contact the secretary: +91 98300 12345
