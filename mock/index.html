          Who can become a member?

          Anyone living in or around Netaji Para. Each member fills the form, and people in the same household can join on one form. The subscription covers the whole household.

          Can non-members come to the pujas?

          Of course. Pujas and the blood donation camp are open to everyone. Only bhog, the picnic and sports day need a member pass.

          How do I book the serving team?

          Pick a package on the catering page and ask for a quote, or come to the club. We serve, we don’t cook. Members pay 10% less.

          How do I pay my subscription?

          By UPI in the member app, or in cash at the club. You get a receipt on WhatsApp for every payment.

                Privacy & terms

                Last updated 13 November 2026

                What we keep

                What the membership form asks: your name, photo, date of birth, blood group, mobile, email (optional), address and only the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar; the names and relation of family joining on your form; and your subscription and receipt history. We never store a full Aadhaar number.

                Why

                To run membership, receipts, event passes and the AGM accounts. Blood group is only looked at when a blood donation camp needs it.

                Who can see it

                Only committee members who need it for their job: the treasurer sees accounts, volunteers see only gate passes. Nothing is sold or shared outside the club.

                Your choices

                See and change your details in the app’s Settings. If you leave the club, ask and we delete your details, keeping only the accounts the law requires.

                Terms

                Member cards are not transferable. If one is lost, tell the club; the old QR is cancelled and a new card is issued.

                Questions, or want your details deleted?Contact the secretary, Rajib Banerjee. We reply within 30 days.
